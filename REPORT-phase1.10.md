## Still UNTESTED

Added by Addendum P (no code):

- **P2: whether a real facilitator lands a payload that went through a redirect to a third party.** The agent no longer sends one; what a facilitator would do with such a payload was not tried.
- **P3: whether any real wallet (MWA, Seed Vault) replaces the blockhash in `signTransactions`.** The guard now refuses it; if a real wallet does it routinely, the payment fails closed and the wallet integration needs a decision (it was invisible before).
- **The principal re-grants between the gate check and settlement (the P7 window).** The resolver gives `mandate_superseded` (G1) or `presented=false` (G2), so the seller can never be liable for that payment. This is a design question, not changed.
- **Gate instances that share one seller key with separate receipt stores** could sign two receipts if a facilitator reports one settlement twice, which reads as equivocation against an honest seller.
- **Clock skew wider than `settleMarginSeconds`** between the gate, the facilitator and the chain.

Carried over:

- **The window is shrunk by 120 s at each end by default**: a mandate shorter than 240 s can never be used through an enforce gate, and a seller whose handlers take longer than the margin can still be held liable. 120 s is a choice, not a measurement of any handler.
- **Two services on one store: a claim holder that crashes after it signed** keeps the signature as pending; the status check then decides, as before. The 150 s grace period is the same constant as before and is not measured against a real network's confirmation time. Tested with two services in one process sharing a file, not with two processes.
- **A malformed amount is refused by shape, not by the facilitator's behaviour**; what x402 does with an amount such as `1e3` or `007` in a payload was not tried on a real facilitator.
- **The raw-path rule refuses paths whose WHATWG form differs for any reason** (a space or non-ASCII character that the client sent unescaped, a tab); such a request is refused in enforce mode though no route confusion was shown for it.
- **The priority bucket, the warm lane and the restart cap** (Phase 1.9) are tested against a local node and a fake clock, not against a public node; an attacker with several accounts can still fill the warm lane; `maxScanRestartsPerHour` is a default.
- **Root was tested in a Linux arm64 container** (`node:24-bookworm` on Docker Desktop), not on native root on macOS and not on x86 CI.
- **The rollback guard is per directory, not per grant**; an unknown grant is 503, never 403; `close({ timeoutMs })` keeps the lock on timeout.
- one keyed provider, one afternoon (Phase 1.7); the mainnet public default is the devnet node's measured one by host name; the empty-page `before` case behind a load balancer; a real lagging history node on devnet; power loss for the directory fsync; held reservations count against the current period whatever period they were reserved in; `verdict_id` is a reference, not a signature; a wSOL account and a Token-2022 grant on chain; the Seeker-signed grant (Phase 2); third-party sellers; anything on mainnet; the period budget is not enforced on chain, only `total_cap` is (scenarios 16 and 16b).
